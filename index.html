Active Wallet Scanner

Streams validated transactions from quaiscan.io's public API and counts unique wallet addresses seen in the last 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days — entirely in your browser, no backend.

Methodology: pages backward through GET /api/v2/transactions?filter=validated on quaiscan.io using keyset pagination, reading each transaction's sender (and optionally recipient) address into per-window Sets, stopping once a transaction older than 30 days is reached. "Active wallet" = an address that sent (or, with the toggle on, also received) at least one transaction in that window.
